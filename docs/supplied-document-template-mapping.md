# Supplied School Document Template Mapping

The uploaded paper documents were treated as **layout and workflow references only**, not as a source of live school, family, applicant, or billing records.

| Paper pattern | Reusable NSOS template interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Branded letterhead with school name, strapline, address line, and contact line | Tenant-controlled document header with optional strapline, address line, and contact line; global defaults remain anonymous. |
| Admission form with pupil details, guardian details, optional background details, medical notes, and declaration | A protected public admissions form with a fixed safe optional-field catalogue and an editable declaration. |
| Termly fee slips grouped by learning-stage categories | A reviewable editable fee guide with sample band labels and amounts. Adoption creates **draft fee structures only**; it never creates invoices or charges a family. |

The source documents show early-years, primary, junior-secondary, and senior-secondary fee bands. Those values are only a starting sample in the application and remain editable by each school owner or administrator before they can be adopted.
