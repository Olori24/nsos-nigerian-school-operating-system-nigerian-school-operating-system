**Status:** Ready for human review; not sent or delivered.

**Offer:** First three months free for eligible schools, subject to written pilot scope and terms.

## 3. Phone opening

Good day. My name is Bolaji Akande, calling from NSOS.

We sent an enquiry to the school and received an acknowledgement. I am following up to request a short management conversation, not to interrupt your work with a long sales presentation.

We are building a school operations platform and would like to understand how Covenant Child Academy coordinates its campuses and existing systems. Could you direct me to the person responsible for school operations or digital systems?

If transferred:
- Ask whether now is a suitable time for a 30-second explanation.
- Explain that the first meeting is discovery plus a short demonstration.
- Ask for a suitable 20-minute slot and preferred meeting channel.
- Confirm the person's role and email address before sending a calendar invitation.

## 4. If they ask, “What exactly does NSOS do?”

NSOS is designed to bring school administration and academic workflows into one connected platform. Depending on the school's needs and the modules verified as ready, the demonstration can cover areas such as admissions, student/class administration, fees, attendance, academic records, communication and management visibility.

We would first understand what Covenant Child Academy already uses. We are not asking the school to replace a working system without evidence that doing so is necessary.

## 5. If they ask, “Do you already work with other schools?”

Answer truthfully using only verifiable, current customer references. If there are no approved references to share, say:

“We are currently engaging schools for structured demonstrations and controlled pilot discussions. I would rather show you the product and agree measurable evaluation criteria than make claims we cannot substantiate.”

## 6. If they ask about security or student data

“Our first conversation and demonstration do not require any real student or parent data. Before a pilot involving school records, we would agree the scope, access roles, data responsibilities, security checks and acceptance criteria with the school. We will not ask you to upload student records during an introductory demonstration.”

Do not claim certifications, completed audits, backup guarantees or unrestricted production readiness unless current evidence has been independently verified.

## 7. Meeting close

“Would Tuesday or Thursday be more convenient for a 20-minute online session? I can send a short agenda in advance.”

Only offer actual dates/times after checking availability and confirming the prospect's timezone.

## 9. Follow-up discipline

1. Send one personalised follow-up after reviewing the earlier enquiry and ensuring it is not duplicated.
2. If there is no reply, wait a reasonable interval before one polite follow-up.
3. If the school declines or asks not to be contacted, stop outreach.
4. Record actual replies and meeting outcomes; never infer intent from an automated acknowledgement.
