## Verified description

> **NSOS — Nigerian School Operating System** is a Nigeria-first school and learning-operations platform for school administration, academic operations, communication, finance, student management, and supervised AI workflows.

NSOS is designed to give a school or learning organisation a controlled operational workspace. Its product scope includes admissions, student records, academic structures, attendance, results, fee and finance workflows, staff operations, family-facing access, communication, school websites, and bounded AI assistance. Important actions remain subject to appropriate role controls, review, and explicit confirmation.
