# nsos.ng Registrar Comparison Notes

## Registry baseline

NiRA states that .ng registration, renewal, and transfer are handled through NiRA-accredited registrars. The registry policy therefore applies regardless of whether the registrar is Nigerian or international.

## Nigeria-based purchase indication

NG Domain's 2026 pricing guide lists the direct `.ng` extension at **₦13,975 per year including 7.5% VAT**. It identifies `.ng` as the short premium Nigerian identity and lists `.com.ng` at **₦6,450 including VAT** as a lower-cost alternative.

WhoGoHost's local registrar guidance states that anyone can register a `.ng` name without a Nigerian address, that registration can run from one to ten years, and that the registrar account manages DNS, hosting, email, renewal, and contact settings.

NG Domain's renewal guide describes account-based renewal through a domain-management area, reminders, selectable renewal periods, displayed renewal pricing before checkout, and optional auto-renewal. It does not publish a fixed `.ng` renewal amount on that guide.

DomainKing publishes an explicit local `.ng` price of **₦17,500** for both registration and yearly renewal, with free transfer. Its site also identifies NiRA among its registry accreditations and lists local and international payment channels.

## Dynadot purchase indication

Dynadot lists `.ng` registration, renewal, and transfer at **US$10.61 per year**. Its page reports no restrictions, DNSSEC support, a 55-day renewal grace period, and a 28-day restore period. It also states that its listed prices can vary with currency exchange rates.

## Operational conclusion

Both paths must use an accredited registrar and can supply DNS records required to point NSOS and verify a Resend email sender. A Nigerian registrar is generally simpler for naira billing and local support; Dynadot offers equal headline registration and renewal pricing in US dollars with established DNS tooling. Final availability and checkout pricing must be confirmed in the registrar cart before purchase.
