# NERDC Curriculum Source Notes

**Retrieved:** 20 August 2026

NSOS will present its Nigeria-first subject and curriculum templates as editable starting points, not as a replacement for an individual school’s authorised scheme of work, examination choices, or curriculum implementation decisions.

| Official NERDC source | Verified structure | NSOS use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [New Revised Basic Education Curriculum](https://www.nerdc.gov.ng/content_manager/new_curriculum_home.html) | Curriculum pages are available for Primary 1–6 and Junior Secondary School 1–3. | Offer separate editable primary and junior-secondary subject templates. |
| [New Revised Senior Secondary Education Curriculum](https://www.nerdc.gov.ng/content_manager/new_senior_curriculum_home.html) | Curriculum pages are available for Senior Secondary School 1–3. | Offer a separate editable senior-secondary subject-template workflow. |

The official sources establish the level structure. Subject selections, class naming, assessment rules, streams, and schemes of work remain tenant-owned configurations and must be reviewed before NSOS creates school records.

## Accessible NERDC subject-category evidence

The [NERDC e-Curriculum subject structure](https://www.nerdc.org.ng/CurriculumStructure.aspx) listed the following relevant subject headings: **English Studies**, **Mathematics**, **Nigerian Languages**, **Basic Science and Technology**, **Religion and National Values**, **Cultural and Creative Arts**, and optional **Arabic Language**. It notes that schools select the Nigerian language appropriate to their environment and that some subject areas combine themes. NSOS will keep these as editable template choices rather than hard-coding a single compulsory offering for every tenant.
