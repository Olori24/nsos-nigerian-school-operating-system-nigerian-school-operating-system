# NSOS Affiliate Programme Terms

> **Draft — review before use.** I’m an AI, not a lawyer. This is a working commercial and operational draft, not formal legal advice. A Nigeria-qualified lawyer and tax professional should review it before it is published, accepted, relied on, or used to make a payment.

**Status:** Draft only. It is not yet effective and does not enrol an affiliate, create a referral link, bind NSOS, promise payment, authorise a public campaign, or establish a payment obligation.

## 1. Parties and Purpose

These Affiliate Programme Terms (the **Terms**) govern the proposed relationship between **NSOS — Nigerian School Operating System** (**NSOS**, **we**, **us**) and an approved technology influencer, content creator, educator, agency, or other independent promoter (**Affiliate**, **you**).

The programme is intended to allow approved Affiliates to introduce eligible educational institutions to NSOS through approved, disclosed promotional content. It does not create an employment, agency, franchise, partnership, joint venture, or authority for an Affiliate to bind NSOS.

## 2. Effective Date and Eligibility

These Terms take effect for an Affiliate only after NSOS has completed written approval, issued an affiliate identifier or referral link, and the Affiliate has accepted the published final version of the Terms. NSOS may accept or decline any applicant at its discretion.

An Affiliate must be legally able to enter into a contract, provide accurate identity and payment information when requested through an approved process, comply with applicable laws and platform rules, and have an audience and content style suitable for a professional school-operations product. NSOS may limit eligibility by territory, channel, category, or campaign.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning for this draft |
|---|---|
| **Affiliate Link** | An NSOS-issued, revocable referral URL or code assigned to one approved Affiliate. |
| **Referred Institution** | An organisation that reaches NSOS through an eligible Affiliate Link and meets the qualification rules in these Terms. It does not include an individual learner, parent, guardian, staff member, or student. |
| **First Verified Payment** | The first payment actually received and independently recorded by NSOS from a Referred Institution for an eligible paid NSOS subscription, after the applicable refund, cancellation, chargeback, and fraud-review period. |
| **Net First Payment** | The amount of that First Verified Payment actually retained by NSOS, excluding taxes, government charges, refunded or reversed amounts, credits, discounts funded by a third party, payment-provider fees where NSOS chooses to exclude them, and amounts not collected. |
| **Qualified Referral** | A Referred Institution attributed to the Affiliate under the final published attribution rules, which has not been rejected for fraud, duplication, self-referral, misrepresentation, or another exclusion. |

## 4. Referral Attribution

NSOS will assign attribution only through an Affiliate Link or affiliate code that NSOS has issued to the approved Affiliate. A referral is eligible only where the affiliate identifier is captured before the Referred Institution creates its first relevant NSOS account or submits another qualifying intake step, as defined in the published programme rules.

The final published programme must specify the **attribution window**, the treatment of multiple referral sources, and whether NSOS applies a first-valid-source or another published attribution rule. NSOS will not use device fingerprinting, undisclosed cross-site profiling, learner records, guardian data, payment evidence, or sensitive school data to settle affiliate attribution.

NSOS may correct, reject, or remove attribution where records reasonably show an invalid, duplicate, self-generated, fraudulent, manipulated, or non-compliant referral. NSOS’s internal source-of-record is controlling for programme reconciliation, subject to the dispute process in Section 13.

## 5. Commission

Subject to these Terms, NSOS will make a **one-time commission equal to 20% of the Net First Payment** for each Qualified Referral. This is not a recurring commission. It does not apply to renewals, later subscriptions, upgrades, school fees, learner admissions, school-collected payments, invoices not paid, free trials, credits, refunds, reversals, chargebacks, or payment arrangements that do not result in a First Verified Payment.

The commission is earned only after all of the following have occurred:

1. NSOS confirms the Referred Institution is a Qualified Referral.
2. NSOS independently records the First Verified Payment for an eligible subscription.
3. The published refund, cancellation, dispute, and chargeback-review period has expired without a disqualifying event.
4. NSOS completes its fraud, policy, contract, and payment-information review.
5. An authorised NSOS owner or finance reviewer approves the commission for payment.

No commission is owed merely because an Affiliate generates a click, lead, form submission, demonstration request, free account, invoice, trial, registration, or conversation. NSOS may change programme pricing, plans, discounts, eligibility, or availability without creating an obligation for a commission that has not yet been earned under this section.

## 6. Payment Review and Timing

All commission calculations and payments will be manually reviewed and approved in the initial programme release. NSOS will communicate the approved payment method, payment cycle, minimum payout threshold, currency, and supporting documentation requirements in the final programme schedule.

NSOS may withhold, defer, offset, or reverse a proposed commission where a payment is refunded, cancelled, charged back, disputed, unpaid, obtained through fraud or prohibited promotion, or where the Affiliate has not supplied the required payment or tax information. A proposed or dashboard-displayed amount is not a payable commission until approved by NSOS.

## 7. Affiliate Marketing Rules and Disclosures

An Affiliate must use only truthful, current, approved information about NSOS. The Affiliate must clearly and prominently disclose the commercial relationship whenever content is compensated, commission-bearing, sponsored, or otherwise influenced by value received or expected from NSOS. Where a platform provides a branded-content or paid-partnership disclosure tool, the Affiliate must use it when applicable and obtain NSOS’s prior permission before tagging NSOS as required by the platform.[1]

An Affiliate must not:

- misrepresent affiliation with NSOS, a school, a government body, a regulator, or another organisation;
- create fake accounts, school registrations, customer enquiries, referrals, reviews, testimonials, endorsements, traffic, or payments;
- promise earnings, rapid growth, enrollment levels, learning outcomes, accreditation, certification, security results, integrations, support times, pricing, discounts, or features that NSOS has not expressly approved in writing;
- target, collect, or exploit learner, parent, guardian, or minor data;
- use spam, unsolicited bulk messages, misleading links, malware, paid search terms, domain names, social accounts, or ad creative that impersonate NSOS or a school;
- use NSOS brand assets, product screenshots, customer stories, trademarks, or confidential information outside the approved brand and campaign materials; or
- offer rebates, gifts, scholarships, cash, or other inducements on NSOS’s behalf without NSOS’s written approval.

## 8. Privacy and Data Protection

Each party is responsible for complying with applicable data-protection law in its own activities. NSOS will minimise affiliate-attribution data and restrict affiliate reporting to the information needed for programme administration. An Affiliate must not ask potential customers to disclose school operations, learner data, family data, payment evidence, login details, or sensitive personal information to the Affiliate.

Where an Affiliate collects any personal data independently, the Affiliate must provide any required privacy information, identify a lawful basis for processing, honour applicable data-subject rights, and keep the data secure. The Nigeria Data Protection Commission identifies consent, contract, and legal obligation among recognised lawful bases for processing; the final programme privacy notice must state the actual basis NSOS relies on for referral attribution.[2]

NSOS will not share personal student, guardian, staff, or school-operational data with an Affiliate merely to support a commission claim. Affiliates will receive only restricted, aggregated, or pseudonymous programme information needed to administer an approved referral, subject to the final privacy design.

## 9. Fraud, Audit, and Compliance Review

NSOS may monitor programme activity using proportionate, privacy-conscious controls to detect fraud, duplicate attribution, suspicious traffic, self-referrals, impersonation, policy violations, and invalid commissions. NSOS may request reasonable evidence of compliance, pause a link, withhold a proposed commission, or investigate an apparent breach. Nothing in this section authorises NSOS or an Affiliate to access another school’s tenant data or to bypass NSOS security controls.

## 10. Taxes and Records

The Affiliate is responsible for determining, reporting, and paying taxes, levies, social contributions, and similar obligations arising from commission income, except to the extent NSOS is legally required to withhold or report an amount. NSOS may require tax, identity, invoicing, or payment documentation before approving a payout. The final programme must be reviewed for Nigerian tax treatment and any cross-border payment obligations before launch.

## 11. Intellectual Property and Brand Use

NSOS grants an approved Affiliate a limited, non-exclusive, revocable, non-transferable licence to use only the NSOS brand assets and approved programme materials supplied for the permitted campaign. The licence ends automatically when the programme participation ends or NSOS withdraws approval. All NSOS intellectual-property rights remain with NSOS.

## 12. Suspension and Termination

NSOS may suspend or terminate an Affiliate, remove a link, or disqualify a referral immediately if it reasonably believes the Affiliate has breached these Terms, made a misleading claim, failed to disclose a commercial relationship, mishandled data, committed fraud, used prohibited marketing, damaged NSOS’s reputation, or created legal or security risk. NSOS may also end the programme or change its future terms on reasonable notice, subject to settlement of commissions already earned and approved under the then-current Terms.

An Affiliate may end participation by ceasing use of the Affiliate Link and notifying NSOS through the designated programme contact. Termination does not entitle an Affiliate to commissions that were not earned and approved before the effective termination date.

## 13. Disputes and Reconciliation

An Affiliate must raise a written referral or commission query within the final programme’s specified review period, providing only non-sensitive evidence necessary to identify the referral. NSOS will review the request using its privacy-safe programme record and provide a reasoned decision. The final programme must select the governing law, venue, and escalation process after Nigeria-qualified legal review.

## 14. Changes to These Terms

NSOS may amend the proposed programme rules before launch. After launch, NSOS may change future-facing programme rules on notice where lawful, but it should not retrospectively reduce a commission that has already met all earning conditions and been approved. Material changes to the commission percentage, attribution window, qualified referral definition, payment timing, or disclosure obligations should require affirmative acceptance before they apply to an Affiliate’s future activity.
