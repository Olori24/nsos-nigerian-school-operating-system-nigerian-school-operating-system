# Public Entry Refinement — 26 August 2026

## Tester feedback and design decision

A tester confirmed that the NSOS entry was working and asked for further front-end refinement. The focused response is the public sign-in and first-workspace journey, rather than an unscoped visual rewrite of protected school operations.

The revised entry uses a dark, high-contrast product canvas with a clear two-column composition. The left side explains how a new organisation begins and retains the existing Google, passwordless-email, and Manus options. The right side replaces broad numerical claims with a factual three-step operating path: create a secure workspace, connect the work that matters, and grow with a clear record. It explicitly includes schools, vocational centres, coaching programmes, online providers, and corporate academies without inventing customer counts, outcomes, testimonials, or learner data.

The optional PWA install card now waits eight seconds before appearing. This preserves installability while reducing first-visit competition with sign-in and the global appearance control. No tenant records, data routes, permissions, or sign-in semantics are changed by this presentation refinement.
