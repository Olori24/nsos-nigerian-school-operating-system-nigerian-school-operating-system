# NSOS Brand Asset Visual Check — 22 September 2026

The generated `nsos-icon-512.png` was visually checked after rasterization. The first generation exposed an oversized canvas with the mark confined to the upper-left quarter; the rasterizer was corrected to render against a fixed 64-unit design canvas, and the icon set was regenerated. The corrected 512px icon fills the square cleanly, preserves the dark green NSOS palette, shows a readable school-roof and learning-pillar mark, and has no clipping or unwanted text. The same generator produces the 16px, 32px, 180px, and 192px variants.
