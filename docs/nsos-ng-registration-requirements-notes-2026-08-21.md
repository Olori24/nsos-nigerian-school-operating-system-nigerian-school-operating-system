# nsos.ng Registration Requirements Notes

## Registry channel

NiRA requires applicants to submit .ng registration and renewal requests through a NiRA-certified registrar; applicants cannot submit a request directly to NiRA. The registrant must confirm acceptance of the NiRA Registrant Agreement, and registration information must remain complete, accurate, and current through the registrar of record.

## Direct .ng versus restricted labels

DomainKing lists direct `.ng` as **Generic** and lists `.sch.ng` and `.edu.ng` as **Restricted**. Its direct `.ng` product page provides an immediate Register action, while the restricted education labels use a separate How to Register path. This supports treating `nsos.ng` as a standard direct-domain purchase, not an educational restricted-domain request.

## Information and documents

The official NiRA policy requires registrant legal-type and administrative-contact information. The current direct `.ng` product information reviewed did not publish a required government-ID, CAC-certificate, or school-document upload for the generic direct `.ng` extension. The older general NiRA policy contains Nigerian-content requirements for closed labels, so those requirements should not be assumed for the generic direct `.ng` name.

## Safe purchase preparation

Prepare the legal registrant name, legal type, responsible administrative contact, valid email, phone number, and physical/postal address. Enter an organizational registrant where the domain should belong to the NSOS business rather than a personal account. Confirm the exact document list in DomainKing’s cart before paying, because a registrar may perform additional anti-fraud or payment verification.

## DomainKing pre-checkout observation

The public DomainKing search accepted `nsos.ng` and redirected to a checkout URL carrying the requested domain name. Its checkout panel did not expose availability, registrant fields, or price in the unauthenticated session, so final availability and all checkout-specific documentation requests must be verified after account sign-in and before payment. No cart, order, or payment was created during this inspection.

## Independent accreditation check

NiRA’s official business directory lists **HOSTAFRICA** under **Accredited Registrars** and provides the exact DomainKing registration URL (`https://www.domainking.ng/domain-registration/`) as that listing’s website. NiRA also publishes `support@hostafrica.com` and phone `+234 1 227 8926` for the listing. This independently supports that the specific DomainKing registration page is tied to a NiRA-accredited registrar identity.

Accreditation is not a guarantee against payment fraud or an account compromise. A safe purchase should still use only the exact URL published by NiRA, verify the TLS address bar and cart price, avoid payment links sent over chat, and use a payment method with transaction protection.
