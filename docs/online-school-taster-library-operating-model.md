# NSOS 19-Prompt Online School Taster Library

## Product position

The **19-Prompt Taster Library** gives a prospective school, vocational institute, coaching centre, or online-training provider an immediate demonstration of NSOS value. An owner chooses a niche and NSOS fills a comprehensive launch prompt that enters the existing private Single-Prompt Online School Launcher.

> The selling point is not an unsupported promise of instant public revenue. It is the ability to turn a credible learning idea into a reviewable internal programme, curriculum, milestones, materials, tutor brief, and readiness evidence in one controlled flow.

## Included prompt niches

| Learning domain | Included tasters |
|---|---|
| Digital skills | Digital literacy, social-media content planning, Excel records, data analysis foundations |
| Creative skills | Canva visual design, mobile photography, short-form video editing, creative makeup |
| Business operations | Sales and customer care, project coordination, product discovery |
| Practical trades | Home baking, fashion sewing, salon service, catering operations |
| STEM and enterprise | Coding for young beginners, solar-energy literacy, agribusiness record keeping |
| Academic support | Study skills and exam-preparation habits |

Each prompt gives the structured learning planner a declared audience, delivery style, practical focus, desired learning value, request for non-graded materials, AI-tutor configuration brief, and a configuration-readiness check. The model output remains strict-schema validated and is persisted only after the owner/admin performs the explicit draft-only approval.

## Buyer experience

An owner opens **Automation Desk**, selects **19 launch-ready course tasters**, optionally filters by niche, and chooses **Use this full taster prompt**. The resulting detailed prompt is visible and editable before NSOS creates a job. The owner then reviews the short job plan and the proposed internal course blueprint. One approval saves only the existing programme, module, milestone, and material drafts in the current tenant.

| Included with a completed private taster launch | Deliberately not included |
|---|---|
| One draft programme, internal modules, human-reviewed milestones, and internal materials | Public website or course publication, domain/DNS action, advertising, or social posting |
| A tutor-scope brief and human escalation reminder | Tutor account creation, automated grading, learner completion, certificates, or credentials |
| A configuration-readiness record with durable counts | Fabricated learners, guardians, staff, applications, reviews, testimonials, attendance, payments, invoices, receipts, or messages |
| Human-review prompts for later pricing and delivery design | Fees, billing, payment collection, bank accounts, payment-provider configuration, or revenue claims |

## Monetisation conversation

The library helps a business test breadth of demand and demonstrate rapid course-creation capacity. It prepares material that a proprietor can review before choosing a commercial model, such as cohort delivery, subscription access, live classes, self-paced content, or supported practice. NSOS does not represent that a selected taster has a price, learner demand, payment setup, public availability, sales outcome, accreditation, qualification, employment result, or revenue. Those are separate business decisions and verified workflows.

## Safety and trust boundary

The library is tenant-local, rate-limited, owner/admin-gated, and uses no personal data. Raw user prompts are not stored in audit metadata. Every taster includes language requiring a private launch, non-graded material, human review, and the exclusion of people, finance, messaging, credentials, and public claims. The configuration-readiness check evaluates only the internal records created by the same approved job; it is not synthetic learner or financial testing.
