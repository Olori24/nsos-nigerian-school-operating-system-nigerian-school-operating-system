> **Draft — review before use.** I’m an AI, not a lawyer or tax professional. This is a working operational and commercial pack, not formal legal or tax advice. A Nigeria-qualified lawyer and tax professional must review it before it is published, accepted, relied on, used to collect partner data, or used to approve a payout.

**Status:** **Internal review only.** This pack does not enrol a partner, create a referral identifier, publish terms, contact a creator, enable tracking, connect a payment provider, collect payment instructions, send outreach, make a payout, or make a public claim.

## 1. Proposed Programme Baseline

The owner-recorded internal defaults are the proposed starting point, not an effective contract or payment entitlement. A Qualified Referral would be an eligible institution introduced through one approved, revocable partner identifier. It would exclude learners, parents, guardians, staff, individual student admissions, and school-collected fees.

| Item | Proposed internal default | Launch condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commission | 20% of the institution’s first verified **net** payment | Final eligible-plan and net-payment definition reviewed. |
| Attribution | 30 days | A first-valid-source or another conflict rule is written and approved. |
| Hold | 30 days after the verified payment | Refund, cancellation, dispute, and chargeback treatment is approved. |
| Minimum payout | ₦10,000 | Manual payment method and payee-verification process are approved. |
| Payout review | Monthly, manual | Finance owner and reconciliation evidence are assigned. |
| Eligible offer | Standard paid NSOS subscriptions only | Specific plans, discounts, taxes, fees, and exclusions are listed. |

> A click, lead, enquiry, account, trial, invoice, school fee, learner admission, or unverified payment is **not** a commission event.

## 5. Referral, Privacy, and Data-Minimisation Controls

The future referral journey should use one opaque, revocable identifier. A public destination must carry a clear referral notice before an institution provides information. Referral data should be separated from tenant school records and must never be used to inspect a learner, guardian, staff, school operational record, or individual school-collected payment.

| Allowed minimum data | Not permitted |
| --- | --- |
| Partner identifier, approved destination, referral timestamp, consent-notice version/state, lifecycle state, duplicate/fraud flag, and audit-safe reviewer decision. | Passwords, raw session tokens, device fingerprinting, learner/guardian information, student admissions, individual school fees, detailed tenant records, sensitive free text, or unrestricted partner analytics. |

The final privacy notice must state the real processing purpose, lawful basis, retention period, contact route, data-subject rights process, and deletion/revocation procedure. It must be reviewed before collection begins.

## 6. Fraud, Refund, and Chargeback Control Register

| Risk signal | Manual control | Required outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-referral or related-party referral | Compare only proportionate institution/contact/payment evidence under the approved privacy process. | Pause attribution; record reason; allow review. |
| Duplicate institution claim | Apply the final approved attribution conflict rule. | Accept one valid source or reject all per documented evidence. |
| Impersonation or prohibited claims | Review the content and partner identity; preserve only necessary evidence. | Suspend/revoke immediately where necessary; require correction. |
| Invalid traffic or fake activity | Review aggregate/limited event evidence; do not use cross-site profiling. | Reject referral or pause link; document manual decision. |
| Refund, cancellation, dispute, or chargeback | Recheck the verified payment after the final 30-day hold. | Do not approve a payout while disqualifying events remain open. |
| Complaint or privacy request | Route to named owner/legal/privacy contact under the final process. | Acknowledge, investigate, restrict data access, and record closure. |

## 7. Manual Payout Operating Procedure

This procedure starts **after** a future named partner is approved, a referral identifier is active, an eligible institution has paid, and the approved hold period has ended. It does not authorise payment now.

1. The finance reviewer creates a calculation sheet from the platform billing source of record, using the approved net-first-payment definition and a frozen calculation snapshot.
2. The reviewer checks partner agreement version, attribution record, eligibility, refund/cancellation/chargeback status, duplicate/self-referral flags, prohibited-promotion review, and the final tax/payee-document checklist.
3. The finance reviewer proposes a payout only if the eligible amount meets the ₦10,000 minimum. The proposal includes no learner, guardian, tenant operational, or unnecessary payment information.
4. A separate owner/finance approval records the exact proposed recipient, amount, currency, method, evidence references, and consequence. No automated wallet, transfer, or commission payment is permitted.
5. After an authorised payment route is chosen and separately confirmed, the finance reviewer records the external settlement reference and a minimal audit entry. A disputed, refunded, charged-back, or incomplete payment must be paused or reversed according to the final terms.
