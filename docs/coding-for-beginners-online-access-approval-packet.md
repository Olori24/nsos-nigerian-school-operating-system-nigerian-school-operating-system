## No-email student portal assessment

The current NSOS sign-in model uses Google OAuth or passwordless email verification. A student profile can exist without an email address, but a portal identity without an email address cannot authenticate through either supported method. Creating such a user record would therefore create an unusable account and weaken auditability; it would not give the learner secure access.

The secure no-email option currently available is the existing guardian portal route: the authorised guardian accepts the already-sent invitation using the guardian email, then accesses the learner’s authorised family information through the linked guardian portal. If the school requires a learner-owned portal rather than guardian access, the school must first provide a learner-controlled email address or separately approve a new secure, supervised identity method with its own recovery, consent, rate-limit, audit, and account-linking controls. No such identity method has been created by this review.
