# NSOS AI Education Business Engine — Delivery Contract

**Status:** Active phased delivery plan  
**Scope:** Owner-controlled school, training-centre, coaching, online, hybrid, and corporate-academy operations  
**Product principle:** **Describe → prepare → review → approve → execute through protected workspaces → audit.**

## Purpose

NSOS is evolving beyond a conventional learning-management interface toward an **AI education business engine**. The goal is to let a non-technical owner describe an education business and receive a coherent, editable institution foundation: learning, public-presence, offer, operations, and growth readiness.

> “AI builds and operates it” means that AI can prepare structured, reviewable work and surface real operating evidence. It does **not** mean that the system may silently publish, message, charge, enrol, grade, complete, certify, alter a provider or domain, or make an individual academic, admissions, or financial decision.

## Non-negotiable controls

Every new workflow must preserve the following contract.

| Control | Required behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Tenant isolation | Every tenant-owned read and write is constrained by `schoolId` and active server-side membership. |
| Role boundaries | Owner/admin controls are enforced by protected procedures, not hidden navigation alone. |
| Approval | Consequential writes require an explicit confirmation at the protected route. |
| Audit privacy | Audit metadata records bounded event facts, never raw owner prompts, learner evidence, review notes, secrets, or other private text. |
| Data truthfulness | Recommendations use either owner-supplied context, reviewable source references, or current tenant aggregates. They cannot invent people, revenue, conversion, demand, student outcomes, credentials, or social proof. |
| Academic integrity | AI may coach and prepare non-graded drafts; a human retains authority for assessments, grades, results, completion, and credentials. |
| External effects | Publishing, domain/provider changes, messages, campaign spend, payments, enrolment, and credentials remain separate reviewed workflows. |
| Automation | A private review cue is not a schedule. No in-process timer, background loop, or unattended consequence is introduced. Future recurring work requires a separately deployed, idempotent, owner-managed job design. |

## Acceptance evidence for the next increment

The next release must demonstrate, without synthetic learners, payments, campaigns, testimonials, or business metrics:

1. An owner/admin can prepare one tenant-private brand, offer, and growth plan from an existing Builder prompt.
2. The plan is explainable, editable where appropriate, and carries clear review and protected-workspace handoffs.
3. No plan write can publish a website, create a logo asset, set a price, create a learner, capture a lead, send a message, collect a payment, activate an enrolment, issue a record, or schedule background work.
4. Tenant, role, confirmation, rate-limit, audit-privacy, no-side-effect, and mobile regressions pass.
5. TypeScript, production build, and the full suite are run; any external integration failure is reported unchanged rather than bypassed.

## Explicitly deferred work

Public certificate verification, unattended lifecycle messaging, live campaign execution, affiliate/referral payouts, automatic payment actions, individual retention or purchase predictions, individual academic recommendations based on sensitive performance, automatic course completion, and autonomous account creation are not part of this release. Each needs separate data, product, privacy, legal, integration, and approval design before it can be considered.
