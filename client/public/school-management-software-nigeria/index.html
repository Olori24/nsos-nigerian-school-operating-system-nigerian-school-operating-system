SCHOOL OPERATIONS · NIGERIA

School management software for Nigerian schools

NSOS is a web-based school operations platform designed to bring everyday administration and academic work into a more connected workspace. Schools can configure the workflows they use and manage access within their own school environment.

One connected workspace for school operations

School administration involves people, records, learning activities, finance and communication. NSOS brings supported school workflows into one platform, helping school teams work with organised records rather than disconnected processes.

Admissions and student records

Manage applicant information and student records through school-configured workflows.

Attendance and academic records

Record attendance and manage academic information, including results published by the school.

Fees and family communication

Support fee-related administration and give linked families access to permitted updates and records.

Designed around school control

NSOS is built for separate school workspaces. School teams control their operational records and decide which information is shared through the available parent and guardian experiences. Public admissions forms can be configured for a school's own process.

NSOS does not claim that every workflow is automatically enabled for every school. Product setup, available modules and rollout depend on the school's configuration.

A practical starting point

Schools considering a new management platform should begin by mapping their current administration, academic and communication needs. NSOS can then be assessed against those requirements before a school begins onboarding.

We recommend a guided discussion before migrating real student or staff data. Do not submit sensitive school records through a public enquiry form.

Common questions

What is NSOS?

NSOS is a web-based school management and operations platform developed for Nigerian schools.

Which school processes does NSOS support?

The platform includes workflows for admissions, student records, attendance, academic results, finance and parent or guardian communication. Exact availability depends on configuration.

Can a school use NSOS without onboarding?

A school should first discuss its requirements and complete the appropriate setup. Do not assume a workspace is ready for live data before onboarding and security checks.

How can a school contact NSOS?

Use the NSOS get-started page or email hello@nsos.top to discuss the school's needs.
