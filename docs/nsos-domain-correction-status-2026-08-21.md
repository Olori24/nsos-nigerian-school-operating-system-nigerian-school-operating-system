# NSOS Custom-Domain Correction Status — 21 August 2026

## Confirmed position

`nsos.edu.ng` cannot be activated for NSOS as presently structured. DomainKing’s support response confirms that `.edu.ng` is restricted to approved and accredited Nigerian educational institutions and requires incorporation documentation, accreditation documentation, and an institutional authorization letter for NiRA verification. NSOS is a school-operations SaaS platform rather than an accredited educational institution, so these documents must not be submitted.

## Registrar policy and correction route

DomainKing’s published policy states that registered domains are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled, deleted, or changed. Its refund policy separately lists domains as non-refundable. This makes an ordinary refund or direct domain substitution unlikely, though the account holder can still ask DomainKing in writing for a goodwill account credit or another documented exception because the restriction prevents activation.

The support request should ask for a written confirmation that `nsos.edu.ng` will remain pending without the ineligible documents, together with a clear answer on whether DomainKing can offer any credit, cancellation exception, or account-level remedy. It must not ask for activation and must not attach or create inaccurate institutional documents.

On 21 August 2026, DomainKing opened support ticket **#738576** (email reference **#556391**) for this correction request. DomainKing subsequently confirmed in writing that it can cancel the pending `nsos.edu.ng` registration and move the amount paid into the account credit balance for another domain extension. Its separate billing response confirmed that `nsos.ng` is available for **₦17,500 per year** and that registration using the credit will be handled by the billing team.

The account holder explicitly authorized the correction request on 22 August 2026, and the reply was sent successfully through the original support ticket. It asks DomainKing to cancel `nsos.edu.ng`, apply the paid amount to account credit, use the credit toward one year of `nsos.ng`, and confirm any remaining balance before the registration proceeds. No payment or registration completion is authorized until DomainKing provides that final billing confirmation.

## Recommended compliant domain

DomainKing’s live public search confirmed on 21 August 2026 that `nsos.ng` is available at **₦17,500 for one year**. The registrar identifies `.ng` as an unrestricted Nigerian presence domain, and it is a concise, credible brand match for the Nigerian School Operating System. The same search showed `nsos.com.ng` at ₦6,449 for one year as a lower-cost fallback.

No replacement should be placed in a cart, paid for, or registered without the account holder’s explicit confirmation. Once a compliant domain is registered and DNS control is available, NSOS can attach the website domain and verify `notifications@<domain>` with the email sender service to repair invitation-email delivery.
