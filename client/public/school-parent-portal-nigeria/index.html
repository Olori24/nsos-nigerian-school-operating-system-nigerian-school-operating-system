School-controlled visibility

The portal is not an unrestricted view of school records. It is designed to show information already permitted to the linked family record. For example, results are shown only after formal publication by the school.

Schools should verify family-to-learner links, user access and privacy settings before inviting guardians to use the portal.

Better communication starts with reliable records

A parent portal is only useful when the information behind it is accurate. Schools should keep attendance, fee records, published announcements and academic records current, and provide a clear channel for families to report discrepancies.

Common questions

What can parents see in the NSOS portal?

Depending on permissions and available records, linked families can see attendance, invoice and fee activity, published announcements, published results and payment-evidence review decisions.

Can parents see results before the school publishes them?

No. The portal displays results only after the school formally publishes the report cards.

Can a parent view another learner's records?

The portal is designed to show information permitted to the linked family record. Schools must maintain correct family links and access controls.

Does the portal replace direct communication with the school?

No. It supports school communication and record visibility; families should still contact the school when clarification or action is needed.
