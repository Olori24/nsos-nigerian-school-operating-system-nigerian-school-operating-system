# Builder-First Phased Institution Contract

## Product promise

NSOS should feel simple to an institution owner:

> **Describe the learning institution you want. NSOS prepares the work. You approve the consequential decisions.**

The School Builder is the primary entry point for owners and administrators. It replaces a configuration-first journey with one private, reviewable institution blueprint. The blueprint must contain meaningful draft content, not empty placeholders, while it must never pretend that an unverified public, financial, identity, or credential action has happened.

## Complete private blueprint

| Blueprint area | Private output required | Separate owner-controlled action |
|---|---|---|
| Institution direction | Name suggestion, positioning, target learners, mission, vision, and a concise value proposition. | Legal identity, public claims, contacts, registration, and publication. |
| Learning catalogue | A flagship programme plus coherent module, lesson, practical project, and human-reviewed milestone recommendations. | Programme activation, instructor assignment, cohort creation, learner enrolment, delivery calendar, and completion. |
| Assessment readiness | Low-stakes formative-practice and assessment-design recommendations with review guidance. | Assessment activation, grade settings, score entry, result approval, and result publication. |
| Student experience | Orientation, support, practice, project, feedback, and next-step recommendations. | Student account creation, registration, enrolment, learner-specific decisions, and messages. |
| Website readiness | Unpublished headline, introduction, programme callouts, FAQ, and owner review notes. | Public website publication, media, domain/DNS, admission visibility, and public claims. |
| Admissions readiness | Suggested published-information checklist and owner decisions. | Public form configuration, applicant data, reviews, decisions, admissions letters, guardian records, and enrolment. |
| Revenue readiness | Free/paid/bundle/subscription decision prompts and transparent missing-decision list. | Prices, discounts, coupons, invoices, payment collection, provider settings, bank changes, and commercial activation. |
| Lifecycle readiness | Owner-visible ideas for welcome, inactivity support, completion celebration, and next-programme follow-up. | Message delivery, automated account actions, credential issuance, alumni status, and campaign execution. |
| Quality readiness | A private completeness report for duplicate titles, missing lessons, missing project/assessment guidance, incomplete handoffs, and launch blockers. | Publishing or declaring the institution launch-ready. |

## Three deliberate states

| State | Meaning | Owner experience |
|---|---|---|
| **Private blueprint** | AI has prepared editable content and readiness guidance. | Review, edit, regenerate a new version, or open protected handoffs. |
| **Internal foundation applied** | An owner has explicitly applied an inactive internal learning foundation. | Review the Learning Centre and activate individual elements through their existing controls. |
| **Publication ready** | The quality report has no required private-content gaps, but external and consequential controls can still remain unavailable or pending. | Review each independent publication, finance, admissions, communications, domain, and provider control. |

`Publication ready` is a review state, never an automatic publication or commercial-launch action.

## Version and regeneration rule

Regeneration creates a **new private blueprint version**. It cannot overwrite an applied programme, published website, active fee, learner state, message history, result, or credential. An owner can edit the new private blueprint and selectively apply supported drafts after confirming each action.

## Hard boundaries

The School Builder does not create people, student accounts, real applications, enrolments, payments, invoices, notifications, public content, web domains, provider credentials, grades, completions, certificates, alumni records, testimonials, reviews, results, or outcome claims.

It also does not determine whether a learner is falling behind, why an individual dropped out, whether a person should pass, which price to charge, or what financial action to take. It may identify generic readiness prompts and route the owner to an existing protected workspace.
