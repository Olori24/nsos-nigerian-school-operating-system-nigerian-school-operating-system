# NSOS Affiliate Activation Plan — Approval First

**Status:** **Planning only.** This document does not activate the affiliate programme, contact a creator, issue a referral link, enable tracking, publish terms, connect a payout provider, make a payment, or create a public marketing claim. It is an operational decision record, not legal or tax advice.

## Purpose

NSOS can test an affiliate channel only as a small, reversible, owner-controlled programme. The purpose is to establish whether individually approved partners can introduce eligible institution decision-makers to NSOS using accurate, properly disclosed content. It is not a commitment to rapid growth, earnings, enrolment results, programme outcomes, or product capacity.

The existing internal pilot defaults are retained as a proposed commercial baseline: **20%** of an eligible institution’s first verified net payment, **30-day** attribution, **30-day** refund/chargeback hold, **₦10,000** payout minimum, monthly manual review, and standard paid-plan eligibility. These defaults are not published terms and do not create any right to commission.

## Non-Negotiable Boundaries

| Boundary | Required control | Not permitted before a separate approval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partner admission | Owner reviews and approves each named partner before any identifier, material, or access is given. | Open registration or self-service affiliate accounts. |
| Data minimisation | Collect only contact, agreement, disclosure, partner identifier, referral timestamp, consent state, and review status necessary for the pilot. | Learner, guardian, school-payment, password, or behavioural-profile data in partner analytics. |
| Public claims | Use an approved claim library tied to demonstrable features and current readiness. | Promises about outcomes, accreditation, results, capacity, savings, support times, earnings, or growth. |
| Disclosure | Require plain-language commercial disclosure and platform disclosure tools where applicable. | Undisclosed paid or commission-bearing endorsements. |
| Money movement | Manual finance/owner review after the verified-payment and hold conditions are met. | Automatic commissions, wallets, transfers, or payouts. |
| Communications | Use a verified, owner-approved sender and recipient list only after the sender-health gate is resolved. | Outreach, onboarding, invitations, or promotional delivery while sender readiness is unresolved. |

The Nigeria Data Protection Commission’s published privacy principles emphasise fair, lawful and transparent processing, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, security, and data-subject rights.[1] Meta states that affiliate-link content should carry the **Paid partnership with** label and that creators must meet its branded-content requirements where applicable.[2] A Nigeria-qualified legal and tax professional must assess the final terms, processing basis, disclosures, and payout treatment before launch.

## Phased Activation Sequence

| Gate | Deliverable | Required evidence | Explicitly excluded action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **0. Launch readiness** | Final terms, partner agreement, privacy notice, disclosure examples, claim library, tax review, dispute and suspension process. | Named Nigeria-qualified legal/tax reviewer; final document versions; retention and deletion schedule; complaint owner. | Publishing or accepting partners. |
| **1. Operating controls** | Role-restricted review workflow, revocation path, audit-safe event model, consent-notified referral route, fraud-review checklist, monthly reconciliation template. | Security and tenant-isolation review; no learner/guardian data in reports; rollback test; owner/finance responsibilities accepted. | Creating live links or tracking public traffic. |
| **2. Partner-by-partner approval** | A limited named cohort, each with reviewed audience fit, agreement, permitted channels, disclosure wording, territory, and removal path. | Signed/accepted final agreement; content draft approved; partner’s disclosure capability checked; owner approval recorded. | Bulk onboarding or mass outreach. |
| **3. Controlled referral activation** | One opaque, revocable partner identifier and one approved destination per partner. | Consent notice on the destination; no sensitive fields in URL or analytics; audit log; quick revoke verified. | Cross-site profiling, retargeting, or attribution to learner/guardian activity. |
| **4. Verified conversion review** | Manual review of an eligible institution’s first verified net payment after any refund/chargeback hold. | Payment evidence, exclusion checks, fraud review, attribution record, finance/owner approval. | Automatic commission accrual or payment. |
| **5. Monthly review** | Aggregate pilot report: partners approved/revoked, referrals, verified eligible conversions, complaints, fraud flags, pending/rejected payouts, and lessons. | Evidence pack reviewed by owner, finance, and legal/compliance contact. | Expansion or public performance claims without separate approval. |

## Required Decisions Before Gate 0 Can Close

| Decision | Owner must choose or provide | Why it cannot be inferred |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal and tax review | Named Nigeria-qualified reviewer, scope, and approval of the final terms. | Commission arrangements, marketing representations, and tax treatment need context-specific review. |
| Programme documents | Terms version, partner agreement, privacy notice, disclosure templates, prohibited-claims list, suspension and dispute rules. | Internal defaults do not constitute a partner contract. |
| Eligible transaction definition | Precisely identify paid plans, net-payment definition, discounts, reversals, refunds, taxes, exclusions, and maximum payout. | “First payment” alone does not resolve edge cases. |
| Payout operation | Manual payment method/provider, finance owner, payee-verification standard, records, currency, and payout timetable. | A commission policy does not authorise a payment method or payee collection. |
| Fraud operation | Self-referral, duplicate-entity, impersonation, abnormal-traffic, dispute, and suspension handling. | Fraud thresholds and appeal rights affect partners and customers. |
| Support and privacy operation | Contact route, response ownership, data retention, opt-out/objection handling, and escalation record. | Partner and referred-visitor data needs accountable stewardship. |
| Launch cohort | Small named initial cohort, individual permitted channels, territory, content, and activation dates. | Cohort size and public exposure must be deliberate and reversible. |

## Approval Packets

Each approval below is intentionally narrow. Approving an earlier packet does not authorise a later packet.

### Packet A — Governance and Terms

> I approve the attached affiliate terms, partner agreement, privacy notice, disclosure examples, eligible transaction definition, fraud policy, complaint process, and Nigeria-qualified legal/tax review. I understand that this approval does not activate a partner, publish a programme, issue a link, or make a payout.

### Packet B — Pilot Controls

> I approve implementation of the named internal referral-review, consent, revocation, audit, and manual-reconciliation controls. No public link, partner, tracking, outreach, payout provider, or payment is authorised by this approval.

### Packet C — Named Partner

> I approve the named partner, their signed/accepted agreement, approved content, disclosure wording, permitted channel, territory, and revocation path. I approve issuance of one revocable referral identifier only; no payment, public claim change, or additional partner is authorised.

### Packet D — Verified Conversion and Payout

> I approve manual review of the presented eligible conversion. I confirm that attribution, payment verification, refund/chargeback hold, fraud checks, exclusions, tax treatment, and payment instructions have been reviewed. This approval is limited to the stated payout decision.

## Current Recommendation

NSOS should remain **internal-only** until Packet A is complete. The responsible next action is to obtain Nigeria-qualified legal and tax review, complete the owner decisions above, and then return with the exact document versions and a small named pilot proposal. No partner, referral link, tracking, outreach, contract, provider, or payout should be activated from this plan alone.
