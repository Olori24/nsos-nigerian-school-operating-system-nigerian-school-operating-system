ADMISSIONS WORKFLOWS

Online school admissions for Nigerian schools

A clear admissions process helps schools collect applicant information consistently and review applications through a defined workflow. NSOS includes public admissions forms connected to each participating school's admissions setup.

Talk to NSOSExplore the platform

A school-configured application experience

Public admissions pages are generated for schools using their configured admissions profile. The form can present the school's identity, admission title and configured fields, while collecting applicant and parent or guardian contact details.

Additional information and supporting evidence can be requested according to the school's configured admissions template. The school remains responsible for its admission criteria and review decisions.

From submission to school review

1. Applicant details

Collect applicant names and parent or guardian contact information through the public form.

2. Supporting information

Capture configured supplemental fields and permitted document evidence where requested.

3. School review

Submissions are routed into the school's admissions workflow for authorised staff to review.

Protect applicant information

Admissions data can include personal information about children and families. Schools should collect only information required for their process, communicate their privacy practices clearly and restrict access to authorised personnel. Test the complete admissions workflow before inviting families to submit real information.

Common questions

Does NSOS provide online admissions forms?

Yes. NSOS includes public admissions forms connected to a school's configured admissions profile and template.

Can schools customise the information they request?

The public admissions form uses the school's configured admission template and fields. Exact configuration options depend on the school's setup.

Who reviews submitted applications?

Applications are routed into the school's admissions workflow for authorised school staff to review.

Does NSOS decide which applicants are admitted?

No. Admission decisions and criteria remain with the school.
