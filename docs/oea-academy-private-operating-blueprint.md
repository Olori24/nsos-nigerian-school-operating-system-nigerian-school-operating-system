# OEA Academy Private Operating Blueprint

**Status:** Owner-review draft. This is a configuration blueprint, not a launched institution, legal policy, public catalogue, payment offer, accreditation claim, or promise of outcomes.

> **Proposed identity:** **OEA Academy — Open Education & AI Academy**. The name, logo, public contact details, website, programme activation, pricing, policies, merchants, domains, admissions visibility, and certificate rules require an owner-selected tenant and separate protected approval.

## 1. Academy direction

OEA Academy is proposed as an online, practice-led learning institution for African entrepreneurs, professionals, and aspiring digital builders who want to develop responsible capability in AI, automation, software, data, digital growth, and modern work. Its learning philosophy is **learn by understanding, practising, receiving accountable feedback, and applying skills in meaningful projects**. It does not promise jobs, income, business results, professional licensing, government recognition, university status, or accreditation.

| Design area | Private draft direction | Owner decision required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mission | Make practical digital and AI learning more accessible through structured, human-reviewed learning experiences. | Confirm final public wording. |
| Vision | Support learners to build confident, responsible capability for a changing world of work. | Confirm claims and audience. |
| Learners | Beginners through early practitioners, with clear prerequisite routes and accessible text-first support. | Confirm age groups, jurisdictions, safeguarding, and eligibility rules. |
| Delivery | Online, asynchronous-first learning with optional human-reviewed cohorts or live support where legitimately staffed. | Configure programme delivery, instructors, cohorts, calendars, and support. |
| Brand | Calm, credible, modern, educational, and practical. A logo concept must be owner-selected and rights-cleared. | Select logo, colours, typography, and licensed assets. |
| Public identity | Disclosed use of supervised AI study assistants and human accountability for consequential educational decisions. | Confirm contact, support, privacy, and policy details. |

## 2. Private five-programme catalogue

The following catalogue is designed for owner review in Course Studio and Institution Builder. Each programme is an editable **internal draft**, not a public course, fee, credential, or available enrollment.

| Programme | Intended foundation | Four proposed courses | Practical capstone direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI & Automation Foundations | AI literacy and safe workflow design for beginners. | AI Concepts for Everyday Work; Prompting with Purpose; No-Code Workflow Design; AI Automation Project Lab. | Design a responsible, documented automation for a real workflow. |
| Data Analytics for Decision-Making | Data thinking and interpretation for non-specialists. | Spreadsheet Analysis Essentials; Data Cleaning and Questions; Dashboards and Narratives; Applied Analytics Project. | Turn an approved small dataset into a decision-ready briefing. |
| Software Development Launchpad | Software-building fundamentals and collaborative problem solving. | Web Foundations; JavaScript Problem Solving; APIs and Application Design; Build and Explain a Small App. | Build, document, and demonstrate a small reviewed web application. |
| Digital Growth & Marketing Systems | Responsible audience, content, and measurement practice. | Digital Audience Research; Content Systems; Campaign Planning and Measurement; Growth Experiment Project. | Prepare a reviewed campaign plan with a clear measurement hypothesis. |
| Digital Entrepreneurship & Future of Work | Commercial problem framing, operations, and technology-enabled work. | Opportunity Discovery; Digital Business Models; Productivity Systems; Venture Operations Project. | Present a responsible launch or improvement plan for a legitimate venture idea. |

Each programme should begin with an owner-approved audience, level, prerequisites, duration, accessibility note, assessment design, material rights check, and human review path. Course Studio can generate a bounded internal module/milestone/material draft, but the owner must edit and confirm individual programme records before activation.

## 3. AI role charter

Every AI capability must be visibly disclosed as an **AI assistant**, not an employee or a substitute for accountable staff. The charter below maps requested academy functions onto the existing approval-first architecture.

| Disclosed AI role | Allowed responsibility | Never allowed | Escalation and audit boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI Principal Assistant | Summarise owner-visible readiness, aggregate workload, launch blockers, and permitted recommendations. | Change finances, grades, certificates, ownership, payments, security, or provider settings. | Owner opens a protected workflow; insight source/limit and handoff are recorded. |
| AI Admissions Guide | Explain approved public programme information and guide a visitor to the configured form. | Invent requirements, decide eligibility, admit, enroll, or send reminders independently. | Human admissions reviewer handles every decision. |
| AI Course Designer | Prepare editable course/module/material drafts from owner-approved inputs and sources. | Publish, activate, create people, claim accreditation, or create a credential. | Owner/admin reviews and confirms affected internal records. |
| AI Tutor | Explain configured learning topics, model reasoning, offer safe examples, and suggest practice. | Grade, answer live assessments, infer sensitive traits, change progress, or issue outcomes. | Teacher-support escalation and tenant/user scope apply to every response. |
| AI Student Success Assistant | Surface learner-owned practice guidance and aggregate review cues where evidence exists. | Diagnose, label risk, message learners, or initiate intervention autonomously. | Accountable staff reviews support needs and communication preferences. |
| AI Marketing Assistant | Prepare editable copy or campaign concepts. | Publish, spend, capture leads, or make outcome claims. | Owner approval precedes any external campaign workflow. |
| AI Finance Assistant | Explain owner-visible readiness or route to Finance. | Set prices, collect funds, alter payments, issue refunds, or change merchants. | Finance/owner completes each protected monetary action. |
| AI Support Assistant | Answer approved FAQs and identify a need for human help. | Access unrelated tenant data, resolve disputes, or handle legal/security complaints autonomously. | Escalate financial, legal, security, safety, and requested-human cases. |

## 4. Policy and learner-experience readiness

NSOS can prepare owner-review copy directions for admissions, refunds, privacy, terms, acceptable use, conduct, academic integrity, AI disclosure, support, and certificate criteria. These are **not legal advice** and must be reviewed for the selected business entity, operating jurisdiction, payment provider, learner age range, data-protection obligations, and delivery model before public publication.

The learner experience should be mobile-first and text-accessible: programme overview, next permitted lesson, practice support, reviewed project evidence, transparent progress context, and a clear route to accountable human support. AI tutor interactions must have text fallback, appropriate usage limits, and no requirement to use voice.

## 5. Owner-controlled launch sequence

| Stage | What OEA owner reviews | What NSOS may prepare | What remains blocked until separate approval/evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Choose tenant | Exact institution to use or create through the authenticated workflow. | Private Builder/Studio drafts only. | Direct database creation, unapproved rename, or cross-tenant import. |
| Establish learning foundation | Catalogue, module/material drafts, project directions, evidence sources, tutor scope. | Inactive internal programmes, modules, milestones, and materials. | Activation, staff/instructor assignment, learners, progress, assessment results. |
| Establish policies and quality | Owner-approved policy text, safeguarding, completion and issuer criteria. | Private review checklist and controlled policy draft where supported. | Legal validity, public policy display, automatic completion or credential issuance. |
| Establish public presence | Website copy, FAQ, programme descriptions, AI disclosure, published-information requirements. | Editable unpublished website draft. | Publication, domain/DNS, public catalogue, testimonials, or claims. |
| Establish commercial readiness | Programme pricing intent, refund/review rules, merchant/provider readiness. | Missing-decision list and protected Finance handoff. | Payment activation, charges, enrollment after payment, refunds, or provider changes. |
| Conduct launch validation | Controlled role checks, provider tests, security evidence, staged synthetic load. | Factual launch scorecard. | Real payments, messages, external campaigns, capacity claim, or paid public launch. |

## 6. Deliberate exclusions

The blueprint does not create a customer-facing “demo school” with invented people or outcomes. It does not include testimonials, case studies, learner reviews, claimed completion rates, fabricated certificates, fake revenue, synthetic payment success, fake support history, or sample admissions. It also does not create a legally binding refund/privacy/terms document, public certificate verification service, merchant account, sender domain, voice service, or automated high-impact AI workflow.
