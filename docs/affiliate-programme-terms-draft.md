# NSOS Affiliate Programme Terms — Draft for Review

**Status:** Draft — review before use, publication, partner acceptance, or implementation. This document does not activate the programme, create an affiliate account, issue a referral link, record a referral, calculate a payable commission, or authorize a payout.

**Important:** I am an AI, not a lawyer. This is a working commercial draft, not formal legal advice. A Nigeria-qualified lawyer and tax professional should review it before NSOS relies on, publishes, or asks any partner to accept it.

## 1. Programme and acceptance

The NSOS Affiliate Programme is a proposed platform-level programme for approved technology influencers, educators, content creators, and other partners who introduce eligible schools or learning organisations to NSOS. A person or organisation becomes an Affiliate only after NSOS approves the application, verifies the permitted promotional channels, confirms the required disclosure language, and records acceptance of the final terms version.

NSOS may accept, reject, suspend, or terminate an application at its discretion, subject to applicable law and any final dispute process approved for the programme. No applicant may represent that they are an approved Affiliate until NSOS has issued written approval through the designated programme process.

## 2. Proposed recurring commercial model

The owner’s proposed commission is **20% of every eligible verified payment received from a school referred by an approved Affiliate**. This is a recurring-commission proposal, not an activated entitlement, and becomes operative only after the programme is commercially approved, professionally reviewed, and explicitly activated by NSOS.

The final terms must define **Eligible Payment** before any programme launch. In particular, the final decision must state whether the 20% is calculated from gross receipts or net receipts after explicitly listed exclusions; whether it covers renewals, upgrades, add-ons, taxes, credits, refunds, reversals, chargebacks, payment-processing costs, and promotional discounts; and how long an Affiliate remains entitled to a recurring commission for a referred school. A registration, application, trial, invoice, promise to pay, or gross contract value is not itself a commission event.

The proposed attribution window is **30 days** from the eligible referral event. The proposed refund and chargeback review hold is **30 days** after each verified payment. The proposed minimum payout threshold is **₦10,000**, with a proposed **monthly manual review and payment cycle**. These values remain subject to final owner, legal, tax, finance, privacy, communications, and payout decisions.

## 3. Attribution and privacy

NSOS may use an opaque referral code or parameter on an approved NSOS referral surface. Attribution must be recorded only after the applicable privacy notice is presented and only to the minimum extent necessary to reconcile a potential commission. NSOS must not use fingerprinting, cross-site tracking, detailed browsing histories, learner or guardian data, private school records, payment credentials, uploaded documents, or message content for affiliate attribution.

An Affiliate may receive only an opaque referral identifier and a non-sensitive lifecycle status such as captured, under review, qualified, not eligible, expired, approved, or rejected. NSOS will not disclose a referred organisation’s private operational, learner, family, financial, or billing information to an Affiliate.

## 4. Promotional conduct and disclosures

An Affiliate must clearly disclose the commercial relationship wherever required by applicable law, platform rules, or the final NSOS disclosure standard. The disclosure must be prominent, understandable, and presented near the relevant recommendation. The Affiliate must not imply that a school, government body, regulator, examination authority, or learner has endorsed the Affiliate or NSOS unless NSOS has approved that exact claim and the claim is supported by current evidence.

An Affiliate must not make unapproved claims about accreditation, certification, security, integrations, capacity, learner outcomes, earnings, pricing, support response times, rapid growth, guaranteed results, or features that are not available. Spam, impersonation, misleading advertising, prohibited paid search, cookie stuffing, forced clicks, fake leads, self-referrals, duplicate attribution, and traffic manipulation are prohibited.

## 5. Qualified referral and commission review

A referral is potentially qualified only when it is attributable to an approved Affiliate through the approved referral surface, is not a duplicate or self-referral, is not fraudulent or prohibited traffic, and results in an eligible organisation making an Eligible Payment within the final attribution window. Qualification is not automatic.

NSOS may create a reviewable commission case after independently verifying the payment and applying the final exclusions. A commission remains subject to manual finance review, owner approval, the refund and chargeback hold, fraud review, and the final payment process. NSOS may correct or reject a case where later evidence shows that the referral or payment was not eligible. No commission is payable merely because an Affiliate reports a lead or because an organisation creates an account.

## 6. Payment process and taxes

The proposed programme uses manual review rather than automatic payout. The final terms must state the payment method, currency, required payment details, payment schedule, invoice or documentation requirements, applicable withholding or tax treatment, and the process for failed or returned payments. Affiliates are responsible for providing accurate information and for their own tax obligations unless applicable law requires NSOS to withhold or report an amount.

NSOS must not request unnecessary bank or identity information through ordinary programme records. Any approved payment-information process must use a separately protected channel, access control, retention rule, and reconciliation procedure reviewed by finance and privacy owners.

## 7. Suspension, termination, and disputes

NSOS may suspend an Affiliate identifier or referral activity when it detects suspected fraud, self-referral, duplicate attribution, misleading claims, missing disclosures, impersonation, spam, prohibited advertising, chargebacks, or a breach of these terms. Suspension must be reversible where appropriate and recorded with a safe reason code. NSOS may revoke an active referral identifier and may reject or void a pending commission when eligibility is not established.

The final terms must identify the support route, response expectations, appeal process, governing law, venue, limitation language, and any mandatory consumer or commercial protections. Until those clauses are reviewed, this draft should not be presented as an enforceable public agreement.

## 8. Intellectual property and content permission

An Affiliate receives no ownership of NSOS trademarks, logos, product materials, school content, learner content, or customer records. Any permitted use of NSOS brand assets or product claims must follow the approved content and disclosure review. NSOS may require correction or withdrawal of content that is inaccurate, outdated, misleading, or not approved.

## 9. Changes and final activation gates

NSOS may prepare revised terms, but a material change must receive a new version identifier and any required legal, tax, privacy, finance, and communications review before activation. No referral link, public programme page, partner contract acceptance, provider connection, tracking, payment integration, or payout may occur until the final decision record and explicit activation confirmation exist.

| Decision gate | Required before activation |
|---|---|
| Commercial | Final recurring commission basis, duration, eligible payments, gross-or-net treatment, attribution window, exclusions, threshold, cycle, and currency |
| Legal and tax | Nigeria-qualified review, final agreement language, disclosure, tax and withholding treatment |
| Privacy | Referral notice, lawful basis, retention, data-subject process, and analytics access control |
| Finance | Named reviewer, payment evidence standard, reconciliation, fraud escalation, and manual payout control |
| Communications | Approved claims, creator disclosure language, permitted channels, and correction process |
